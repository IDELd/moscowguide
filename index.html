Arbat Street

Arbat Street is located in the centre of Moscow, just west of the Kremlin. It is a famous pedestrian street full of souvenir shops, cosy cafes, art galleries and bookstores. Here you can walk among old colourful buildings, listen to street musicians, watch artists painting portraits and buy traditional Russian gifts. In the evening the street becomes lively, so it is a perfect place to relax and enjoy the atmosphere.

Lights · Romantic

Nikolskaya Street

Nikolskaya Street is situated in the very centre of Moscow, a few steps from Red Square. This bright pedestrian street is famous for its elegant architecture, souvenir shops, cafes and restaurants. You can walk under hundreds of glowing lanterns, take amazing photos, taste Russian food and buy gifts. In the evening the street shines with colourful lights, making it one of the most romantic and relaxing places in the city.

📍 Centre 🏮 Lanterns 🍽 Food 📸 Photos
Main Avenue · Lively

Tverskaya Street

Tverskaya Street is the main avenue in the centre of Moscow. It runs from Red Square towards the north and is full of shops, department stores, cafes, hotels and theatres. Here you can go shopping, enjoy a cup of coffee, visit a museum or walk and admire the historic buildings. Tverskaya is always busy and lively, so it is a great place to feel the rhythm of Moscow.
